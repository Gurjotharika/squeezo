FAQ

Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Squeezo has no network access. Every tool runs on your Mac.

Will it make my files worse?

Squeezo uses constant-quality encoding and checks the output. If a result would be larger than the original, it keeps the original.

What happens to my originals?

You choose. Save a copy, save to a folder, or replace the original. Replaced originals go to the Trash and you can undo from the app.

Does Enlarge use AI?

No. It resamples and sharpens. It gives cleaner edges than a plain stretch, but it does not invent detail.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Is there a Windows version?

No. Squeezo is built for the Mac.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases go through the App Store, so you can request a refund from Apple.
