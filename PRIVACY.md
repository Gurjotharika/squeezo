# Squeezo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

Squeezo works entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What the app does with your files.** Files you open or drop in are read and processed on your device only.
Nothing is uploaded. Results are saved where you tell the app to save them. Text and face recognition, background
removal and every other tool use features built into macOS and run locally.

**What the app stores.** Only your settings (such as quality and output choices), the list of folders you chose to watch,
and a counter of how many files you have processed today. These stay on your Mac.

**Analytics and tracking.** None. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own.

**Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
Apple's own privacy policy applies to purchases.
